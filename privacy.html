Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Shorts Review is a private tool used only by Benjamin Piwcewicz, the owner of the DarkConfessions YouTube channel. It uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the user agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies.

What data it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the tool accesses only the owner's own YouTube channel. It uploads and schedules the owner's own videos, and reads the owner's own channel and video statistics (views, watch time, likes, comments, shares and subscribers gained) through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API.

How data is used and stored

The data is used only to upload the owner's videos and to produce a performance report for the owner. Sign-in tokens and reports are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. No cookies or tracking are used.

Deleting data and revoking access

The owner can delete the stored tokens and reports at any time. Access can be revoked at any time from Google's security settings: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. All stored data is deleted within 30 days of access being revoked.

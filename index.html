Shorts Review

Shorts Review is a private tool used only by the owner of the DarkConfessions YouTube channel. It runs on the owner's own computer and uses YouTube API Services to:

  • upload and schedule the channel's own original videos, and
  • read the channel's own analytics to compare how different video formats perform.

It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.
